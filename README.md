# Sales and Profitability Analysis | Microsoft Excel

## Project Overview

This project presents an end-to-end sales and profitability analysis developed entirely in Microsoft Excel. The objective is to transform raw transactional data into meaningful business insights by examining sales trends, profitability, product performance, regional performance, and potential future sales development.

The analysis is based on **9,994 sales records** and uses Excel's data preparation, analytical, visualisation, and forecasting capabilities to identify business trends, evaluate performance, and highlight areas requiring improvement.

## Project Objectives

- Analyse historical sales and profit performance.
- Evaluate product and sub-category profitability.
- Identify high-performing and underperforming products.
- Examine regional and city-level sales performance.
- Investigate the relationship between sales, profit, quantity, and discounts.
- Apply forecasting techniques to estimate future sales.
- Translate analytical findings into practical business recommendations.

## Project Structure

The workbook is organised into seven analytical sections:

**1. Raw Data**

Contains the original transactional dataset, preserved as the source for subsequent preparation and analysis.

**2. Prepared Data**

Contains the structured dataset used throughout the project, with standardised fields and prepared date values.

**3. Statistical Analysis**

Provides an overview of sales and profit distribution, revenue classification, product lookup functionality, and statistical indicators. It also includes interactive elements for exploring individual products and business performance.

**4. Product Performance**

Examines sales, profit, and quantity across product categories and sub-categories. It also investigates performance across customer segments and discount levels to identify profitable and loss-making product groups.

**5. Regional Sales Analysis**

Evaluates geographical performance at regional, state, and city levels, highlighting differences in sales, profit, and profitability across markets.

**6. Projections**

Analyses historical sales and profit trends and applies linear extrapolation and exponential smoothing (ETS) to estimate future sales. Historical validation comparisons are used to evaluate forecasting performance.

**7. Summary**

Brings together the main findings and presents business recommendations based on the product, regional, and forecasting analyses.

## Key Analytical Findings

- Total sales reached approximately **$2.30 million**, generating approximately **$286,397 in profit**.
- Annual sales and profit demonstrated an overall upward trend between 2014 and 2017.
- Product profitability varied considerably across sub-categories, with some high-sales products generating negative profit.
- Certain geographical markets recorded negative profitability despite reporting substantial sales.
- The forecasts produced by both methods indicate that the business may continue to experience sales growth over the next two years.

## Business Recommendations

Based on the analytical findings, the project identifies several areas for potential improvement:

- Review pricing and discount strategies for loss-making product categories.
- Investigate the factors affecting profitability in underperforming cities.
- Prioritise products and sub-categories demonstrating strong profitability.
- Use sales projections to support inventory and resource planning while monitoring actual performance against forecasts.
